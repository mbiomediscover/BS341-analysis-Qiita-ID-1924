This project was for my dissertation, submitted June 2026 and graded a First. The project aimed to reanalyse data from an old paper that looked at community changes in the guts of a small cohort of Clostridioides difficile patients who had recurrent cases, despite antibiotic treatment. These patients were given faecal microbiota transplants (FMTs), deemed a 'last resort' intervention for persistent gut infections that often harbour antibiotic resistance genes.
I also aimed to reproduce findings while examining the methodology. For example, rarefaction curves were generated to support discussion around the trade-off between sacrificing data and getting even sequencing depth. I used a few packages that I came across but this analysis could definitely be added to with more time (we were given 5 weeks to do the entirety of research and writing for our dissertations and this was my first time working with R). 
The source paper and data can be found on the site Qiita with ID '1924'.
